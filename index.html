Pame Volta: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Pame Volta ("the app") shows public electric-vehicle charging stations in Cyprus on a map. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing about you.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send any personal data to us.

Favourites and your car

Chargers you save as favourites, and the car and plug types you enter, are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us, and deleting the app removes them.

Location

If you tap the location button, the app asks for permission to use your location while the app is open. It uses your location only on your device, to show where you are on the map and to sort chargers by distance. Your location is never stored or sent by the app.

Map

The map is drawn from OpenStreetMap data, loaded from OpenFreeMap's servers (openfreemap.org). Like any website, those servers receive your device's IP address and which part of the map is being shown, so that they can send the right map pieces. They do not receive your location or any personal details from the app.

Directions

If you choose "Directions", the app opens Waze, Google Maps or Apple Maps with the charger's location, and those apps' own privacy policies apply. To show a street address for some chargers, the app may use your device's built-in address lookup, which sends only the charger's coordinates.

Searching for a place

When you search for a place or address (for example a venue or a street), the words you type are sent to Apple's map search, built into your iPhone, to find it. Apple's privacy policy applies to that search. We never receive what you search for.

Emails you send

"Report a problem", "Suggest a missing charger" and "Send feedback" open your email app with a pre-filled message. We only receive what you choose to send, and use it only to reply and to correct charger data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when optional accounts are added, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
